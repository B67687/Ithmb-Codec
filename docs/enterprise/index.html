Key Differentiators

Each processing profile is individually tuned using Rust SIMD intrinsics (SSE, AVX2, AVX-512, NEON) for the specific sample rates, bit depths, and channel counts encountered in forensic audio.

Benchmarked against libsndfile and SoX on identical hardware. Batch conversions that take 45 seconds in SoX complete in under 6 seconds with matching bit-exact output fidelity.

Pre-Compiled Binaries

Static binaries for Linux (x86_64, aarch64), macOS (Intel, Apple Silicon), and Windows (x86_64). No Rust toolchain, no cargo, no build failures — just download and run.

24-Hour Priority Support

IP Indemnification

Enterprise and Site License holders are protected against third-party intellectual property claims related to the use of pre-compiled Ithmb-Codec binaries.

Forensic Chain-of-Custody

Built-in processing manifest that logs every transform applied, with cryptographic hashes at each stage — admissible as evidence in digital forensic workflows.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Q: How does the Enterprise license work — is it per developer or per machine?
Per developer seat. Each individual who compiles, links, or runs Ithmb-Codec Enterprise binaries as part of their work requires a seat. A single developer may install the binaries on multiple machines they control. Site Licenses cover unlimited seats within a single legal entity.
Q: Do you offer pre-compiled binaries for all three platforms?
Yes. Enterprise and Site License customers receive static binaries for Linux (x86_64 and aarch64), macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon), and Windows (x86_64). Binaries are signed and checksummed. We also provide a Docker image for CI/CD pipelines.
Q: What is the exact support SLA?
Initial acknowledgement within 4 hours of ticket submission (24/7). Our engineering team aims for a resolution or documented workaround within 24 hours. Support is delivered through an encrypted ticket portal with optional PGP-encrypted communication for sensitive forensic workflows.
Q: What does IP indemnification actually cover?
If a third party claims that the Ithmb-Codec Enterprise binaries infringe their intellectual property rights, we will defend the claim and pay any resulting damages or settlement, provided you have used the binaries as delivered (no modifications) and are current on your license. This does not cover claims arising from your use of the open-source build in combination with other software.
Q: Is there a free trial or evaluation period?
We do not offer a free trial, because our target users already know the open-source library and can benchmark it themselves. Instead, every Enterprise and Site License purchase comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee — if the binaries do not meet your needs, we refund 100% of the license fee, no questions asked.
Q: How does the Site License renewal work?
Site Licenses are annual subscriptions. Renewal includes continued access to binary updates, priority support, and indemnification. We offer a volume discount on renewals for organizations that renew within 30 days of expiration — typically 15–25% off the list price depending on org size.
